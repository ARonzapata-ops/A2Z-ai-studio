Save, restore, or start over

Each workspace automatically saves its editable audio project in this browser. A downloaded project ZIP includes the original audio and clip settings. This is separate from the finished WAV and is not cloud synchronization.

Local drafts are stored in IndexedDB. Clearing browser data can erase them. ZIP backups can include up to 50 MB of original media. Your files are not sent to an AI provider by this editor.

Playback controls appear here after processing. If automatic playback is blocked, tap Play above.

Project ZIP support: JSZip license. Audio effects and WAV processing run on this device.

Make your sound your own

  1. Open a sound file, find saved A2Z audio, record your voice, or try the demo.
  2. Choose a clip. Drag on its waveform to trim it, or enter times. Use the track and position controls to layer sounds.
  3. Zoom in on the waveform or zoom to your kept section. Move the view to inspect another part.
  4. Try a sound preset, volume, fades, or pan. Use Hear before and Hear after to compare the selected clip. Turn on Loop to repeat it.
  5. Tap Process & preview. Listen, then export WAV.

Split divides the selected clip at the playhead. Delete leaves a gap; Delete & close gap moves later clips on that track. Undo reverses editing steps.

For another device, download the editable audio project ZIP and restore it there. The seven workspaces are local slots, not private accounts.
